Usage guide & code sample

Choose a semantic modifier such as .k-alert--success or .k-alert--danger; keep the title and body separate for clear hierarchy.

<div class="k-alert k-alert--success" role="status">
  <strong class="k-alert__title">Saved</strong>
  <div class="k-alert__body">Your changes are now live.</div>
</div>
