Country flag usage

Use the full country name in the flag filename. Standalone SVG files preserve every flag detail and work with <img>.

<!-- Vietnam -->
<img src="../dist/icons/flags/flag-vietnam.svg" class="k-icon k-icon--flag-vietnam" alt="Vietnam flag" width="24" height="18">

<!-- United States -->
<img src="../dist/icons/flags/flag-united-states.svg" class="k-icon k-icon--flag-united-states" alt="United States flag" width="24" height="18">
