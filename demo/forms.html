Usage guide & code sample

Wrap controls in .k-form-group, pair labels with controls, and use .k-form-feedback for validation messaging.

<div class="k-form-group">
  <label for="email" class="k-form-label">Email</label>
  <input id="email" class="k-input" type="email">
  <span class="k-form-hint">We never share your email.</span>
</div>
